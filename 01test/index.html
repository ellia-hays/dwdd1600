Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

`Digital citizenship means using the internet, technology, and digital devices safely, responsibly, respectfully, and ethically.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use technology, computers, networks, and the internet within an organization or school.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is presenting someone else's words, ideas, images, or work as your own without giving proper credit.

Plagiarism is considered dishonest because it does not give credit to the original creator. To avoid plagiarism, use quotation marks when copying exact words and include citations for sources.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork or academic activities.

In simple terms: Academic dishonesty means breaking academic rules to gain an unfair advantage or misrepresent your work as your own.

What are the rules for AI-generated content?

AI can be a helpful tool, but you need to use it honestly, follow your school's rules, and take responsibility for the work you submit.
