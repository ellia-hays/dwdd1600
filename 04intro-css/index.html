One Step of Bravery

What We Do

You don't have to feel brave before you act. Sometimes you become brave by choosing to act anyway.

One Brave Thing gives people practical opportunities to step outside their comfort zone, build self-trust, and create stronger connections through small acts of bravery.

Why it Works

Based on the research of Albert Bandura, a psychologist at Stanford known for his work on self-efficacy, whose research shows that repeated actions build confidence and reduce fear. This matters because one in ten people will face the isolation of social anxiety in their lifetime (National Institute of Mental Health).

Fear is normal! But when individuals take small, consistent steps, they develop the confidence to move forward despite it.

What Does Bravery Mean?

Bravery
Choosing to take action even when you feel nervous or uncomfortable.
Self-Trust
Believing in your ability to handle challenges and follow through.
Confidence
Growing belief in yourself through practice and experience.
