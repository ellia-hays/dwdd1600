One Brave Thing

30 Day Challenge

The One Brave Thing 30-Day Challenge encourages you to complete one small act of bravery every day for 30 days. Each challenge helps you step outside your comfort zone, build self-trust, and connect with others.

Be brave. Take the step. Do it anyway.

How to Participate

  • Complete one brave thing each day.
  • Try something that pushes you slightly outside your comfort zone.
  • Reflect on how each brave step makes you feel.
  • Keep going, even when a challenge feels uncomfortable.
  1. Day 1: Introduce yourself to someone new.
  2. Day 2: Give someone a genuine compliment.
  3. Day 3: Speak up when you normally wouldn't.
  4. Day 4: Try something you've never done before.
  5. Day 5: Ask someone for help when you need it.
  6. Day 6: Invite someone to hang out.
  7. Day 7: Do something kind for a stranger.
  8. Day 8: Ask someone how they are really doing.
  9. Day 9: Go somewhere by yourself.
  10. Day 10: Share your opinion respectfully.
  11. Day 11: Try something you are afraid you might fail at.
  12. Day 12: Tell someone how much you appreciate them.
  13. Day 13: Start a conversation with someone you don't know.
  14. Day 14: Ask a question instead of pretending you understand.
  15. Day 15: Do something you've been putting off.
  16. Day 16: Set a healthy boundary.
  17. Day 17: Share something you created with someone else.
  18. Day 18: Eat lunch with someone you don't normally talk to.
  19. Day 19: Apologize for a mistake you've made.
  20. Day 20: Ask someone to teach you something new.
  21. Day 21: Attend an event where you don't know anyone.
  22. Day 22: Stand up for someone who needs support.
  23. Day 23: Tell someone how you really feel.
  24. Day 24: Volunteer for something outside your comfort zone.
  25. Day 25: Try something you've always wanted to do.
  26. Day 26: Celebrate someone else's accomplishment.
  27. Day 27: Take the first step toward a goal you've been putting off.
  28. Day 28: Reach out to someone you haven't talked to in a while.
  29. Day 29: Do something that makes you proud of yourself.
  30. Day 30: Reflect on your 30 days and choose one brave habit to keep.

The goal isn't to be fearless. It's to prove to yourself that you can do things even when you feel nervous or uncomfortable. Each small brave thing helps you build self-trust, and with practice, that self-trust can grow into confidence.
